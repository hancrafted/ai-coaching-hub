For design, stack, and UI decisions, the user subscribes to **form follows function** — pragmatic, not precious about bespoke visuals. They will happily accept an opinionated framework's built-in look (e.g. Astro Starlight) if it delivers **fast + high-quality** output, rather than insisting on a custom-built distinctive aesthetic.

**Why:** stated directly while charting the coaching-hub SSG effort — "if we can achieve fast and high quality output with Starlight for my public content, I'm open to it." They're building a coaching side-business with very low traffic and want a sustainable, low-effort foundation, not a design showpiece.

**How to apply:** when recommending stacks/design, lead with function and delivery speed; don't over-invest in bespoke visuals or argue for custom-built UI on novelty grounds. Frame "custom vs opinionated framework" as a quality-bar question ("does the framework's achievable output clear the bar?"), not a uniqueness question. Still hold a real quality bar — form follows function, but the function must be served well. Related: [[project-repo-setup]].
